# 0048. Preact 11 alongside Preact 10

- Status: Accepted (2026-11-26)
- Date: 2026-11-26

## Context

Preact 11 is stable (11.0.0 on 2026-09-29; 11.2.0 on 2026-11-06). We held it back because `@astrojs/preact` 6
asked for `^10.6.5` (ADR 0002). `@astrojs/preact` 6.1.6 now accepts `^10.6.5 || ^11.0.0`, and `@preact/signals`
2.11.4 accepts `>= 10.25.0 || >=11.0.0-0`.

Four packages share the editor's Preact runtime on a site (ADR 0044): Tapestry, the media library, tapestry-seo
and tapestry-menus. They have to accept the same Preact major, or a site gets two.

Preact 11's breaking changes, checked against our code: ESM only; `defaultProps`, `SuspenseList` and
`component.base` gone or moved to `preact/compat` (we use none of them); numbers in inline styles no longer get
`px` (all our styles are strings with units); `JSX.CSSProperties` gone and `useRef()` needs an initial value (two
places in Tapestry's editor, fixed in a way that compiles on 10 and 11); `ref` reaches function components as a
prop and passive-effect cleanups on unmount run at a different time (runtime: covered by the e2e suites).

## Decision

The four packages declare `preact` as `^10.29.8 || ^11.2.0`: sites on Preact 10 keep working, new installs get 11,
and a site settles on one copy (ADR 0044). The lower bounds are versions we tested (ADR 0047). Each repository's
lockfile runs 11.2.0, so unit tests and CI test Preact 11; the published releases before this one tested 10.29.8.
The playground runs 11.2.0.

Verified before release: typecheck, unit tests and build of all four on 11.2.0 (Tapestry also on 10.29.8), and
the playground's 32 e2e suites with all four as `file:` builds and one Preact 11.2.0.

## Consequences

- Sites can move to Preact 11 (and `@astrojs/preact` islands on 11) whenever they like.
- New Preact code must compile on 10 and 11 while the range includes 10: no `JSX.CSSProperties`, explicit
  `useRef` initial values. Drop 10 from the range in a later release once nothing needs it.
- Release order follows the dependency graph: media library, Tapestry, then tapestry-seo and tapestry-menus (their
  dev dependencies must be releases that accept 11, or their lockfiles carry a second Preact).
